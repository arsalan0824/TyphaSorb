Cattails absorb 14 times their weight in oil

with a 95% retention rate.

20,000+ small oil spills occur in the US annually

causing up to $40,000 in remediation fees and local ecological damage. Historical events such as Deepwater Horizon show that cleanup is slow and causes long-term economic and environmental harms.

Marinas currently rely on polypropylene booms or harmful chemical dispersants.

Such booms are expensive ($100-$300/unit), non-biodegradable, non-reusable, and create a secondary plastic waste effect rather than cleaning the environment.

Our Cattail mechanical solution uses raw fibers compacted into a pod.

These pods are extremely lightweight and can absorb the oil without soaking the water, allowing for mechanical pressing to recover the clean oil for reuse. Unlike synthetic alternatives, our pods are biodegradable, abundant, can be reused 5 times, and allow for oil recovery.
